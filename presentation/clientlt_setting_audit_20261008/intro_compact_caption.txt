English caption
With the same global long-tailed sample pool and training strategy, Client-LT concentrates tail-class samples in a shared small client group and exhibits a larger late-training accuracy decline. (a–b) Actual sample counts for all 30 clients and all 20 tail classes, with the original client order and a shared color scale; white cells contain no samples, and the outlined three-client group in (b) holds 119/153 (77.78%) of the tail samples. (c) Unsmoothed accuracy over rounds 0–100: Client-LT falls from its peak of 71.70% (star) to 59.55%, a decrease of 12.15 percentage points (pp), whereas class-wise Dirichlet falls from 73.05% to 71.25%. Both runs use the same CIFAR-100-LT training pool (10,847 samples, IF=100), rank-4 CLIP LoRA with logit adjustment, full participation, split/training seed 42, and strategy J: three local epochs jointly updating LoRA factors A/B per round, plus one epoch updating A at rounds 10,20,...,90, with sample-weighted aggregation. Client-LT uses lambda_T=0.75, alpha_T=0.5, q_T=0.1 and rho=3; class-wise Dirichlet uses beta=0.5; the observation is specific to this configuration, with other update strategies reported separately.
